How the Project Works (6 Key Steps)

1

Select Target Crop

Farmer selects their specific crop from 13 supported staple varieties (Apple, Corn, Wheat, Potato, Rice, etc.).

2

Upload Leaf Image

A clear photo of the suspected diseased or healthy leaf is uploaded directly from phone or computer.

3

Instant AI Diagnosis

Trained computer vision models scan leaf patterns in under 15ms to diagnose pathogen or healthy status.

4

Strict Crop Isolation

The system strictly confines predictions to the chosen crop, completely preventing cross-crop confusion.

5

Dual Treatment Advisory

Provides comprehensive treatment lines including chemical sprays with exact dosages alongside organic bio-remedies.

6

Interactive AI Assistance

Farmers can chat with the built-in AI Agronomist for follow-up guidance on spray timing, fertilizers, and field care.
